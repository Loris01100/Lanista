# AGENTS.md

This file provides guidance to AI coding agents (Codex, Claude Code…) when working with code in this repository.

## Project

Lanista is a gladiator-management game written in Rust (solo developer, Steam target, Windows + Linux/Steam Deck). The technical roadmap is [docs/Gladiator_Manager_Feuille_de_route_Rust.md](docs/Gladiator_Manager_Feuille_de_route_Rust.md) (in French); it is the reference for stack, architecture, phases and determinism rules. Gameplay rules (fighters, combat formulas, economy, colosseums, competitions) live in the design plan [docs/Gladiator_Manager_Plan_de_conception.md](docs/Gladiator_Manager_Plan_de_conception.md) (in French). Sprite, arena and sound formats and the Aseprite export settings are in [docs/Formats_graphiques.md](docs/Formats_graphiques.md) (in French). Phases 0 to 4 are done (tags `phase-0` to `phase-4`); phase 5 (the world) is done, its milestone checked by `three_seasons_live_without_the_player` in `state.rs` (rival money swelling: taken up in phase 5 bis, with staff hired on the market by the player and the rival clubs, and free agents fighting in the colosseums). Phase 5 bis so far: combat accuracy: a seventh stat, `Stats::accuracy` (PRÉ, `STAT_COUNT`), sets the chance to hit against the target's speed (`combat::hit_percent`, rules in `data/combat.ron` read as `DesignData::combat`; an `Arena` always hits unless built `with_accuracy`, as real fights are; a head injury lowers accuracy, its after-effect too, in `data/fighter.ron`); of two fighters striking in the same tick, the faster strikes first, a draw on the arena's generator settling equal speeds (`combat::cast_order`); a hit may stun by its spell's `damage_kind` (blunt three times as often as cutting, neutral never, `combat::stun_chance`, rules in `data/combat.ron`, none unless the arena is built `with_rules`): the stunned fighter stays down, neither acting nor hit (`Fighter::is_standing`, the AI leaving it alone), then gets up by determination (`combat::rise_percent`, as many times as it can) or is knocked out, a defeat alive (`Fighter::knocked_out`, `EventKind::Stun`/`Rise`/`KnockedOut`), and may keep a severe head wound (`injury.stun_wound_chance`); the model draws knock-outs and stuns too (`Ending::stunned`, `knocked_out`); a cutting hit may instead sever (`combat::severed_part`, `CutRules` in `data/combat.ron`, none unless the arena is built `with_rules`): the head, mostly on a fatal blow, killing with no survival draw, or a limb, taking the fighter out of the fight alive, a defeat, with a permanent injury there (`Fighter::severed`, `EventKind::Sever`, `injury::severed_limb_wound`); the model draws lost limbs and beheadings too (`Ending::severed`); free agents fight in the colosseums (`Contender::FreeAgent`, an index in `GameState::free_agents`, which keeps everyone who came to the market with its status: `Sold` once signed, `Retired` past `max_free_agents`; `GameState::available_free_agents`), registering by the `free_agents` profile of `data/world.ron` as managers without money (`GameState::free_agent_plans`: only where entry is free, never trained nor cared for) and earning Elo, record and fame but no money; rival fighters and free agents are the outsiders, paired in `outsider_weeks` and met by the club's fighters, a free agent champion defending like a rival one (`Colosseum::best_outsider`); a fighter's Elo and title follow it to another club or the market (`Colosseum::transfer`), saves v11; rival clubs hire a trainer then a doctor (`Rival::staff`, `rivals::staff_moves`: the best candidate, then the cheapest, whose salaries it could pay its profile's `staff_weeks` weeks, the one held kept while it could pay half as many unless a candidate is of a better level; no recruiter, but for a poor club, which hires one first: `rivals::staff_posts`) and pay their salaries each week, their effects applying to their fighters (`rivals::plan` at the staff's prices, `play_week` and `CareerFighter::modelled_fight` taking each side's `Medicine`), saves v12; staff is hired on the market among candidates shared by the club and the rival clubs (`GameState::staff_market`, `staff::Candidate`: a `Person` whose `Member` has a level and its own rules and salary, drawn around its level's by `staff.market` in `data/economy.ron`; a miss spends the spell and is logged (`EventKind::Cast { missed }`, `FighterReport::misses`), and the utility AI weighs casts by their chance to hit; the death rate is reviewed: the most determined give up at 10 % of their HP, and a fighter brought to 0 HP survives half the time (`injury.fatal_blow_survival`) with a severe wound (`injury::fatal_blow_wound`) that replaces the after-fight injury draw (`CareerFighter::take_side`, both in real duels and through the model); every club is poor, modest or rich (`economy::Wealth`, `GameState::wealth`, `Rival::wealth`; `wealth` in `data/economy.ron`: starting money, a patron's weekly gift (`Flow::Patron`), and the money from which a club rises to it, `Wealth::next` moving one level at each off-season start, down below half its level's; the player picks it for a new game, `GameState::with_wealth`, and sees the rival clubs' wealth, never their money), each salary is paid once per 3 fighters in activity (`StaffRules::salary_shares`), a rich rival club buys a better fighter each week from clubs no richer than it and lets go at any age those who no longer perform (`rich_purchases`, `rich_release_percent`, `rich_upgrade_percent`), a poor one signs the free agents of the best potential its recruiter sees (`market::estimated_potential`) and buys from no one, and no club buys below half its level's money, saves v14; awaiting the `phase-5` tag. Phase 5: `lanista_core::colosseum` and `data/world.ron` hold the colosseums (two: Capoue, free, and Rome; arena type without combat effect until phase 6, prestige making the fighters generated for them stronger, purses, Elo start and K); `GameState::colosseums` keeps each one's Elo ranking (melee-ready: `record_places`, `log::places`; those who stop fighting leave it) and its champion, the fighters themselves living in the clubs (`Contender::Club`, `Contender::Rival(RivalId)`, `Rosters`): the title changes hands only in a title fight (`Activity::TitleFight`) and must be defended every 4 weeks (rival champions defend against the best other rival fighter at the end of the week, a club champion past its deadline loses the title; a survived defense pays the champion bonus) and the rival fighters' challenges (`Activity::Challenge(ColosseumId, RivalId)`: one the club sends is accepted if Elo gives the rival fighter its club's `accept_percent`; `GameState::rivals` keeps each club's money and roster, a fighter who leaves keeping its entry; the colosseums' first residents are shared out in turn at the opening; each week `GameState::rival_plans` rests, cares, trains or registers each fighter in the colosseum where its Elo chances are best, the club's fighters meet registered rival fighters, and `rival_weeks` pairs the others by Elo for real duels; `lanista_core::market` holds the market (rules in `data/economy.ron`; `market::value` from stats, fame and record (`CareerFighter::record`); `GameState::free_agents`, signed for nothing, topped up each week, rookies and the release of old rival fighters worth less than their club's average each off-season; `Decisions::moves` holds `Move`s: observe, sign, release, put on or off the transfer list (`GameState::transfer_list`; rival clubs with a place make `SaleOffer`s, accepted the next week, the fighter fighting on until then), and offer a price for a rival fighter, answered at the end of the week by its club (accept, `GameState::counter_offer` or refuse) then by the fighter (`market::refuses_to_join`); `WeekSummary::market` tells what happened; a fighter who leaves the club keeps its entry with `Status::Sold` or `Status::Released`; stats out of the club are hidden or seen as ranges, revealed week after week of observation (`market::stat_ranges`, `CareerFighter::observed_weeks`)), shown in the club screen's "Marché" view; `lanista_core::staff` holds the staff: a trainer, a doctor and a recruiter (`Role`), each with levels 1 to 5 in `data/economy.ron` and a weekly salary paid even into debt (`Flow::Salary`, a `Transaction` with no fighter); the trainer sets the session price and its `Coaching` raises the training progression chance and spares form, the doctor's `Medicine` makes care keep fewer weeks at its price and may spare an after-effect (`injury::spare_after_effect`; `CareerFighter::play_week` takes the `StaffEffects`), the recruiter's `Scouting` reveals more stats a week, narrows the ranges, estimates the potential (`market::potential_range`) and sees the personality (`market::personality_seen`); `GameState::staff`, changed by `Decisions::staff` (`StaffMove`s, the first for each post counting), shown in the club screen's "Personnel" view; the club screen's colosseums, market and staff views live in `club_screen/` (`colosseums.rs`, `market.rs`, `staff.rs`), with the sheet of a fighter out of the club (`View::Outside`: what the club sees, its place in each colosseum, market actions) and a rival club's page (`View::Rival`), opened by clicking names; ## Commands

```sh
cargo build                          # whole workspace
cargo run -p lanista                 # the game executable
cargo run --release -p tools --bin simulate -- --seed 42    # one duel, log and report
cargo run --release -p tools --bin batch -- --fights 10000  # win rate per class, average duration
cargo run --release -p tools --bin career -- --seed 7 --weeks 10  # one fighter's weeks
cargo run --release -p tools --bin calibrate -- --fights 20000    # fit data/stat_model.ron on real duels
cargo run --release -p tools --bin compare -- --fights 20000      # real duels against the model, fails on a gap over --tolerance points
cargo test                           # all crates
cargo test -p lanista_core <name>    # single test (substring filter)
cargo fmt --all --check
cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings
cargo machete                        # unused dependencies
cargo deny check                     # advisories, licenses, sources (deny.toml)
cargo llvm-cov --workspace --lcov --output-path lcov.info  # coverage for SonarQube Cloud, before a local scan
```

On Linux, linking `lanista` needs the ALSA headers (`libasound2-dev`) for macroquad's audio.

Python module `lanista_py` (PyO3, cdylib, no Rust tests): create `python/.venv` once (`py -m venv python/.venv`, then `pip install maturin` inside it), activate it, then `maturin develop -m crates/lanista_py/Cargo.toml` and `python -c "import lanista_py"`.

CI ([.github/workflows/ci.yml](.github/workflows/ci.yml)) runs all of the above; clippy and tests run on both Windows and Linux so determinism breaks show up. Each push to `main` also runs the SonarQube Cloud analysis with coverage (`sonar` job, `sonar-project.properties`, `SONAR_TOKEN` repository secret; automatic analysis is off in SonarQube Cloud). Any clippy warning fails CI, and the `pedantic` group is on for the whole workspace (`[workspace.lints.clippy]` in the root `Cargo.toml`; each crate has `[lints] workspace = true`, which a new crate must add too). Fix a lint rather than silencing it, or use a local `#[expect(clippy::…, reason = "…")]`. Package metadata (edition, authors, repository, `license-file`, `publish = false`) lives in `[workspace.package]` and each crate inherits it with `<field>.workspace = true`; a new crate must do the same. `publish = false` is what lets `deny.toml` skip license checks for workspace crates. The code is proprietary (all rights reserved, see `LICENSE`).

`[profile.dev.package."*"] opt-level = 2` in the root `Cargo.toml` is deliberate: simulations are too slow with unoptimized dependencies in debug.

## Crate naming vs. the roadmap

The roadmap uses placeholder names; the actual crates are:

| Roadmap name | Crate | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gladiator_core` | `lanista_core` | Pure game logic (combat, AI, fighters, world, economy, save) |
| `moteur` | `reesch` | Generic engine (macroquad + egui: loop, sprites, input, audio, screens) |
| `gladiator` | `lanista` | Game binary; wires core + engine |
| `gladiator_py` | `lanista_py` | PyO3 module for RL training (phase 0 prototype for now) |
| `gladiator_ia_onnx` | `lanista_ia_onnx` | ONNX inference of the learned AI with `tract` (phase 0 prototype for now) |
| `tools` | `tools` | Command-line binaries (`simulate`, `batch`, `career`, `calibrate`, `compare`) |

The workspace picks up any `crates/*` automatically.

## Architecture rules (enforced by crate dependencies)

`cargo deny check` enforces the forbidden dependencies below through `[bans] deny` + `wrappers` in [deny.toml](deny.toml). A new crate that may legitimately depend on a banned crate must be added to its `wrappers`; `lanista_ia_onnx`, `lanista_py` and `tools` are listed there.

- `lanista_core` must never depend on macroquad, egui, tract or pyo3 — logic is runnable headless (tools, tests, Python training).
- `reesch` must never depend on `lanista_core`; it stays game-agnostic. Only add engine features a current game phase needs.
- Refer to entities by IDs (`FighterId(u32)`) stored in `Vec`/tables, not references.
- A week is a function (`next_week(&mut GameState, &Decisions)`), a combat step is a function (`tick(&mut Arena, &mut [Box<dyn CombatAi>])`). UI only builds decisions and reads state.
- Logic runs at a fixed tick rate; rendering interpolates. Speed-ups just run more ticks per frame.
- Design data (classes, spells, arenas) lives in RON files under `data/`, not in code. Saves are versioned JSON with migrations.

## Naming

All code is in English: modules, types, functions, variables, constants, test names, code file names, comments. Only player-facing text and the `docs/` documents stay in French. The design plan's French stat abbreviations map to `hp` (PV), `melee_attack` (AC), `ranged_attack` (AL), `speed` (VIT), `melee_defense` (DC), `ranged_defense` (DL).

## Determinism (in `lanista_core`)

Same seed must replay the same combat across Windows and Linux (cloud saves, replays). [crates/lanista_core/clippy.toml](crates/lanista_core/clippy.toml) enforces these with `disallowed-types`/`disallowed-methods`; it applies to that crate only, and replaces any root `clippy.toml` for it.

- No `HashMap`/`HashSet` — use `BTreeMap` or sorted `Vec`.
- Float math (`sin`, `sqrt`, …) via `libm`, not `f32`/`f64` methods.
- One `ChaCha8Rng` per combat seeded explicitly; never `thread_rng()`.
- No wall-clock time (`Instant::now`); logic only knows ticks.
- Parallelize across independent combats (rayon), never inside one.

## Validation

Before handing off code changes, run `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` and the relevant `cargo test`; run `cargo machete` and `cargo deny check` too when dependencies changed. In Claude Code, hooks in `.claude/settings.json` run `cargo fmt --all` after each Rust file edit and the clippy command above before handing off (`.claude/hooks/`); the tests are not run by any hook. Saves are not versioned until the Steam release: a change to the saved types needs no new version nor migration (the `save-version` skill, `.claude/skills/save-version/SKILL.md`, is for after the release). Add dependencies with `cargo add`; roadmap versions are indicative. Prefer `try_from` over `as` for narrowing casts (pedantic flags `as`).

Tests: `proptest` for invariants (HP never above max, a combat always ends), `insta` snapshots for reference combats (a seed always produces the same log). Review snapshot changes with `cargo insta review` and commit the `.snap` files; CI sets `CI=true`, so a missing or changed snapshot fails.

## Git

- Never commit: the developer reviews and commits every change. After changing files, end the reply with a proposed commit message in a code block.
- Commit messages: lowercase type, a space before the colon, short summary: `feat : …`, `fix : …`, `chore : …`, `docs : …`, `test : …`.
- Never tag, push or publish without an explicit request.
- `Cargo.lock` is committed (binary project).
- `.githooks/pre-commit` rejects unformatted code; enable once per clone with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`.
- Git LFS tracks `*.png`, `*.ogg`, `*.wav`, `*.aseprite`, `*.ttf`, `*.otf`; line endings normalized to LF.
- Git convention: `main` always working, commit directly to `main` until the Steam release, then one branch per feature, a tag per phase (`phase-0`, `phase-1`…).
